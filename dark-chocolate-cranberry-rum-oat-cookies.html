Dark Chocolate, Cranberry & Rum Oat Cookies

Makes 12 cookies · Prep 10 min · Bake 10–12 min · Oven 170°C (fan-forced)

Healthier Christmas cookies that are not too sweet, contain no added salt, and use olive oil instead of butter. Perfect for sharing at Christmas parties.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • ¾ cup plain or wholemeal flour
  • ½ teaspoon baking soda
  • ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • ⅓ cup olive oil or avocado oil
  • ⅓ cup pure maple syrup
  • 1 large egg
  • ½ teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 teaspoon dark rum or brandy
  • ⅓ cup dark chocolate chips (70% cocoa or higher)
  • ¼ cup dried cranberries

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 170°C (fan-forced) and line a baking tray with baking paper.
  2. In a large bowl, mix the oats, flour, baking soda and cinnamon.
  3. In a separate bowl, whisk together the oil, maple syrup, egg, vanilla and rum.
  4. Add the wet ingredients to the dry ingredients and mix until just combined.
  5. Fold in the dark chocolate chips and cranberries.
  6. Spoon 12 equal portions onto the tray and gently flatten.
  7. Bake for 10–12 minutes, until just set and lightly golden.
  8. Cool on the tray for 5 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack.
Notes:
• Stores at room temperature in an airtight container for 4–5 days
• Freezes well for up to 2 months
• For crisper cookies, bake closer to 12 minutes
