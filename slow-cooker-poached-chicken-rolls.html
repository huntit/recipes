Poached Chicken Rolls with Lemon and Celery

Made start to finish the day before and lifted straight from the fridge. Nothing to time, nothing to keep hot, and it sits happily on the table through a long afternoon of turns.

What you need

The poach

  • 1.5 kg chicken thighs, boneless and skinless
  • 400 ml water or no-added-salt stock
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp black peppercorns
  • 1 brown onion, quartered

The dressing

  • 200 g whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 3 celery stalks, finely diced
  • 4 spring onions, thinly sliced
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • 3 tbsp flat-leaf parsley, chopped
  • 1 tsp black pepper, ground
  • 1 tsp salt — added at the very end

On the table

  • 8 large sourdough rolls
  • 1 baby cos, leaves separated
  • 2 Lebanese cucumbers, sliced
  • 3 tomatoes, sliced
  • Butter — salted and unsalted

Method

  1. Poach gently

    Lay the thighs in the cooker in an even layer. Add the liquid, bay leaves, peppercorns and quartered onion. The liquid should come about two-thirds of the way up the chicken rather than covering it — you are poaching, not boiling. No salt at this stage. Lid on, LOW, four and a half hours.

    Do not overshoot Thighs are forgiving and an extra thirty minutes will not hurt, but much past six hours the texture turns stringy rather than tender. This is one of the few slow cooker dishes with an upper limit worth respecting.

  2. Drain and shred warm

    Lift the chicken out with tongs into a large bowl, leaving the liquid behind. Discard the bay and onion. Shred with two forks while it is still warm — it pulls apart far more easily than it will once cold. Spread it on a tray and give it twenty minutes to cool before anything creamy goes near it.

  3. Dress it

    Back into the bowl with the celery, spring onion, lemon juice and zest, parsley and pepper. Fold the mayonnaise through a third at a time until everything is coated but not swimming. You may not need all of it.

  4. Split before you salt

    Lift roughly a sixth into a separate bowl and leave it unsalted — the lemon and pepper carry it further than you would expect. Stir the salt through the rest, taste, adjust. Cover both and refrigerate overnight.

    Low-sodium serve The mayonnaise is the real source here, at roughly 500–600 mg sodium per 100 g, so even the unsalted portion carries some. If the limit is strict, dress that portion with Greek yoghurt and extra lemon instead of mayonnaise, or use a reduced-salt mayo. On the table, cheddar and salted butter are the other two to watch — put out a small dish of unsalted butter alongside the regular one.

  5. Serve cool

    Lift both bowls straight from the fridge at about twenty-five past twelve. Split the rolls and set out the lettuce, cucumber and tomato alongside. Lettuce leaf into the roll first as a barrier, then the chicken.

    Two-hour rule Once dressed, this should not sit out indefinitely on a warm day. Bring out what will be eaten within about two hours and keep the rest in the fridge — easy enough to top the bowl up between games.
